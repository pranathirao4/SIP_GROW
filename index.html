psychology The 3 Critical Churn Moments

1. The First Salary Delay (Month 2-4)
When monthly debits coincide with irregular salary credit dates or month-end cash crunches, users face mandate bounce fears. Because Groww's 'Pause' is less obvious than 'Delete SIP', users permanently delete the mandate.
2. First Portfolio Drawdown (Month 4-7)
First-time investors expect compounding immediately. When seeing -3% or -5% XIRR on the Groww home screen, psychological panic triggers a redemption or SIP stop.
3. Bank Mandate Expiry / Re-verification (Month 8-11)
Bank e-mandate renewal notices confuse users. Instead of completing OTP re-auth, users abandon the SIP out of friction.

lightbulb High-Impact Product Interventions for Groww

Smart Pre-Debit Warning (T-24h)
Notify users via WhatsApp/Push 24h prior to SIP debit with a 1-tap "Skip this month" button, preventing bank bounce penalties.
Prominent "Pause for 1-3 Months" Flow
When user taps "Cancel SIP", intercept with "Need a temporary break? Pause for 1-3 months without canceling your mandate."
Market Dip Reassurance Banner
During market corrections, display educational insights on unit accumulation ("You're buying 8% more units today at a discount").
